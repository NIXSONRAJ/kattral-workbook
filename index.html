The Helpful Little Bird
Raju was walking in the park. He saw a little bird on the hot ground. The bird was very thirsty. Raju quickly took his water bottle and poured some cool water into a small leaf. The bird drank the water and chirped happily. "Thank you, Raju!" it seemed to say before flying up into the big green tree.
1. Who was walking in the park?
2. What did Raju pour the water into?
💧 LIFE SPARK: Animals feel thirst and pain just like we do. Always be kind to animals!
